03 · A job in the worker

The worker reads the job's kind, runs that domain, and sorts any failure into one of three bins.

Key insight: only failures that can pass on their own are retried; a model that refused, or a loop the guard stopped, goes straight to the dead-letter queue.

Details
  • Lists: FINAL and TRANSIENT in worker.py; settling in queue/consumer.py.
  • A body that will not parse is dead-lettered with nothing recorded: there is no turn to record it on.
  • Retries, the dead-letter queue and replay: Idempotency.
