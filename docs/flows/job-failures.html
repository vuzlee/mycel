Flow

Job failures

  • A job is not lost: written with its row, retried, or parked (only an unreadable outbox row is dropped)
  • Parked jobs go back with one command
  • Hover a box for its file · click to open it
Why
  • Model calls time out, brokers restart, workers die mid-job
  • A job must be retried when that can help, and parked for a person when it cannot
