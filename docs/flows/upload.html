Flow

Upload a document

  • A file becomes searchable pieces
  • Slow work runs on the ingest worker, one at a time
  • Hover a box for its file · click to open it

Key facts

WhatValue
parse and chunkinfra/documents/parse.py (docling) · chunk.py → passages of ~500 tokens
acceptedPDF, DOCX, Markdown · up to 2 MB · checked by its bytes, not its name
limits200 documents per person · 5 processing at once
statusuploaded → parsing → ready or failed · the page gets the list again on every change
stuckwatchdog every 60 s: parsing longer than 10 min → failed
